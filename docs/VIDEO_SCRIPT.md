# DukaVault AI — Submission Video Script

**Suggested duration:** 45–60 seconds  
**Project:** Private Business Intelligence Assistant for UGREEN AI NAS

## Narration

Meet **DukaVault AI** — a private business intelligence assistant designed for UGREEN AI NAS.

Small businesses manage invoices, receipts, stock sheets, and supplier records across many files. Finding changes and spotting inconsistencies can take hours.

DukaVault brings those records together. Ask a question in everyday language, and it can search authorized documents, connect related information, and explain what it finds.

It follows a clear workflow: find relevant files, extract supported details, compare records, then show the source behind each important finding.

For example, ask: **Why did supplier costs increase this month?**

DukaVault can compare invoices, highlight price differences and possible duplicates, and clearly label anything that needs human review.

DukaVault is designed around authorized NAS files, evidence-linked answers, and human control.

The initial Skill package is prepared. Integration with the official UGREEN tools and NAS validation are the next development steps.

**DukaVault AI — understand your business records with confidence.**

1. Title card: DukaVault AI — Private Business Intelligence Assistant.
